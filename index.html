ReneBank ATM
User Manual

How to power on the machine, use your RFID card, and collect your cash, receipt and card.

Parts of the machine

  • ScreenShows prompts and your options.
  • KeypadDigits 0–9, plus *, #, A, B, C, D.
  • Card slotAbove the keypad. Insert and collect your RFID card here.
  • Cash boxOpen compartment in the middle. Your money comes out here.
  • Receipt slotBottom opening. Your printed receipt comes out here.
  • Power cableWires at the back or bottom. Connect to a power source.

How to use it

  1. Plug in the power

    Connect the power cable to the power source and switch it on. Wait for the screen to light up and show the ReneBank welcome message.

    Power cable

  2. Insert your RFID card

    Hold your ReneBank RFID card and put it into the card slot above the keypad. Keep it steady until the screen confirms the card was read.

    Card slot

  3. Enter your PIN

    Type your PIN password on the keypad. Press the confirm key shown on the screen when you finish. Shield the keypad with your hand so nobody can see your PIN.

    Keypad

  4. Choose your transaction

    Follow the options on the screen and use the keypad to select what you want, such as withdrawing cash and the amount.

    Screen and keypad

  5. Take your money

    The cash comes out into the box below the keypad. Reach in and take it all.

    Cash box

  6. Take your receipt

    The receipt prints and comes out of the slot at the very bottom. Take it and keep it as proof of your transaction.

    Receipt slot

  7. Take back your RFID card

    Your card comes out last. Pull it from the card slot and put it away safely. Do not leave before you have it.

    Card slot

Good to know

  • Never share your PIN. ReneBank staff will never ask for it.
  • Enter the PIN carefully. Too many wrong tries may block the card.
  • Always collect the cash, receipt and card before you walk away.
  • If the screen stays blank, check that the power cable is plugged in firmly.
